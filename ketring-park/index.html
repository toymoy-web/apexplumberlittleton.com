Where Ketring Park Sits In Littleton

Ketring Park is a city park built around a lake, and real estate sites such as Homes.com use the name for the neighborhood nearby. Homes.com describes the park as 55 acres with open fields, wooded areas, walking trails and a 12-acre lake. It places the park on the east side of the Sterne Park area and lists ZIP codes 80120 and 80121.

The Littleton Museum sits on 40 acres adjacent to Ketring Lake. No source we found sets a street boundary for the neighborhood, so call with your address and we will confirm coverage. Homes to the west are covered on our Sterne Park page.

Water In The Basement And Around The House

General plumbing guidance, not a local finding. The points below apply to many Colorado homes. We list them because they fit what is known about this area, and we do not claim they describe your street.

Test The Sump PumpIf your house has a sump pit, pour water in until the float lifts and the pump runs. Do this before spring melt and after heavy storms.
Find The Source FirstWater on a basement floor can come from a pipe leak, a drain backup or groundwater. Each has a different fix, so identify the source before repairs.
Plan For Power LossA sump pump stops when the power goes out unless it has battery backup. Ask about a backup if your pit runs often.
Check The DischargeThe pump is only as useful as where it sends the water. A discharge line that ends too close to the house lets water cycle back.
Flood Risk Is UnverifiedWe have not verified groundwater or flood data for these streets. The City of Littleton and FEMA publish flood maps, so check your own address.
Water Bill ChangesA steady rise in the water bill with no change in use can mean a hidden leak. Leak detection finds it with little cutting.

How A Typical Visit Works

Call (303) 552-3896 and tell us what you see. Standing water, a pump that runs constantly and a damp smell each point to different causes. We confirm coverage and give you a few safe steps to take while we head over.

On site, the technician finds where the water comes from before suggesting a repair. If the work becomes a larger water cleanup, we will say so plainly and tell you what comes next.

Before The Technician Arrives

Shut off the main

If a pipe is leaking, stop the water at the main valve first.

Stay out of standing water

Do not touch electrical items or outlets near water on the floor.

Move belongings up

Lift boxes and furniture off the floor to limit damage.

Note the pump behavior

Tell us if the pump runs nonstop, cycles fast or does not start.

Common Questions From Ketring Park Homeowners

Do you serve homes near Ketring Park?

Yes. We are based in Littleton and serve this part of the city. Call (303) 552-3896 with your street address and we will confirm coverage.

Do homes near Ketring Lake flood?

We have no verified flood history for these streets and will not guess. Check the City of Littleton and FEMA flood maps for your address, and call us if you see water in your home.

How often should I test my sump pump?

Many plumbers suggest a test before spring snowmelt and again after heavy storms. Your pump manual sets the exact steps for your model.

Why does my sump pump run all the time?

A pump that never stops can point to a stuck float, a failing check valve or a steady water source. A technician can find the cause quickly.

Is Ketring Park the same as Sterne Park?

They are neighbors but separate names. Homes.com places the Ketring-Gallup park complex on the east side of the Sterne Park area. Either way, a call settles coverage for your address.
