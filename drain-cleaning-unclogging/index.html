Why Littleton Drains Clog

Littleton sits on Front Range clay soil that expands and contracts with moisture, and that movement is a real, documented driver of shifted or cracked sewer laterals in the city's older neighborhoods. A shifted joint catches paper and debris the same way a speed bump catches traffic, and the clog comes back every few months at the same spot until the joint itself gets fixed.

Mature trees are the other big local factor. Old Town Littleton and the older sections of Columbine Valley have decades-old trees whose roots find their way into clay or cast-iron sewer lines through the smallest joint gap. Once roots are inside a pipe, snaking alone only buys a few weeks before regrowth closes the line again. If your clog keeps returning on a predictable schedule, that pattern itself is a reason to ask for a camera inspection instead of another round of cabling.

Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

A single slow drain, like a bathroom sink that empties gradually, is usually a local clog of hair and soap scum right at that fixture. Multiple drains backing up at once, or water rising in a low fixture such as a basement floor drain or tub when you run the washing machine, points to a main line problem instead. That distinction changes both the tool and the urgency.

Gurgling sounds from a toilet or drain when another fixture runs, a sewage smell near a floor drain, or a lowest-fixture backup are all signs the clog sits on the main line rather than inside one branch line. Those calls get treated as same-day priority, since a fully blocked main line means no drain in the house works until it clears.

How We Find And Clear The Clog

For a straightforward single-fixture clog, a motorized cable (snake) clears hair, grease, and soft debris in one visit. For a main line that keeps clogging, or one where the plunger and a basic cable both fail, we run a sewer camera first. Hydro jetting a line with a collapsed section or a broken joint can push debris into a crack rather than clear it. The camera tells us in a few minutes whether root intrusion, grease buildup, or a structural problem is the real cause.

Once we know the cause, hydro jetting at 4,000 PSI or higher scours the full diameter of the pipe rather than just punching a hole through the clog the way a cable does. That's why it holds up longer against grease and root regrowth. If the camera shows a cracked or offset pipe instead of just buildup, cabling or jetting is a short-term fix and the honest answer is a line repair, which is its own separate job we'll walk you through before recommending it.

What actually changes the price: the method needed (cable vs. hydro jetting vs. camera inspection), how far the clog sits from the nearest cleanout, whether it's a single fixture or the main line, and whether it's an after-hours emergency call. Common Questions

Why does my drain keep clogging in the same spot?

A clog that returns at the same location every few months is usually a sign of root intrusion or a shifted pipe joint, not a one-time blockage. A camera inspection identifies the real cause so you're not paying for the same cabling visit every quarter.

Is hydro jetting safe for Littleton's older pipes?

Hydro jetting is safe for sound clay, cast-iron, and PVC pipe. We run a camera first on any line with a known age or history of problems, because jetting a pipe with an existing crack or collapse can make the damage worse, not better.

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning at night?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends, and holidays, at the same upfront-price standard as a daytime call.

How much does drain cleaning cost?
