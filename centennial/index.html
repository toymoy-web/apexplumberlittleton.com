Centennial, ZIPs 80112, 80111, 80121 And 80122

Centennial is a home rule city in Arapahoe County with 108,418 residents, according to the 2020 Census. It incorporated on February 7, 2001, folding in the former Castlewood and Southglenn communities, so many neighborhoods here predate the city itself.

Centennial borders Littleton directly, which puts its western streets a short drive from our shop. Some addresses in ZIPs 80121 and 80122 still carry Littleton mailing addresses even though they sit inside Centennial city limits.

A City Made Of Established Neighborhoods

Because Centennial incorporated in 2001, most of its homes were built while the area was still unincorporated Arapahoe County. Water and sewer service therefore comes from special districts rather than one city utility.

The city's own district map lists providers such as Southgate Water District, Willows Water District, and the Arapahoe County Water and Wastewater Authority. South Arapahoe Sanitation District also serves parts of Centennial, Littleton, and Greenwood Village. Confirming which district covers your street helps us separate a district-side problem from one inside your home.
