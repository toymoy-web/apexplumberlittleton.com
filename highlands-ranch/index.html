Highlands Ranch, ZIPs 80126, 80129 And 80130

According to the 2020 Census, Highlands Ranch has 105,631 residents, which makes it the largest community in Douglas County. It is unincorporated, so Douglas County governs it rather than a city hall.

Many Highlands Ranch mailing addresses read Littleton, CO, because the Littleton Post Office serves the community's ZIP codes. That is why so many homeowners here search for a Littleton plumber. We are based in Littleton, and the community sits just south of C-470, so we treat it as part of our everyday service area.

A Master-Planned Community Built In Waves

Highlands Ranch was founded in 1981 as a 22,000-acre master-planned community, and it has grown in stages ever since. The earliest neighborhoods are now more than 40 years old, while the southern sections are far newer.

That spread means one street may still have original 1980s water heaters while the next has fixtures installed in the last decade. Water and wastewater service comes from Highlands Ranch Water, formerly Centennial Water and Sanitation District, under a contract with the Highlands Ranch Metro District. Knowing which side of the meter a problem sits on saves time, and we can usually sort that out on the first call.

Landmarks And Neighborhood Context

The Highlands Ranch Mansion, operated by the Metro District, is the community's best-known historic building and a handy reference point when homeowners describe where they live. South of the developed neighborhoods, the Highlands Ranch Conservation Area keeps a large band of land open.

Those open edges matter for plumbing in one practical way. Exterior hose bibs and irrigation lines on the outer streets face real winter exposure, and our frozen pipe repair page explains how to protect them.

What We Get Called For Most In Highlands Ranch

With many homes now between 25 and 45 years old, water heater replacement leads the list, followed by water softener and filtration work. Front Range hard water leaves scale on heating elements and fixtures, so homeowners often ask about treatment at the same visit as a replacement.
