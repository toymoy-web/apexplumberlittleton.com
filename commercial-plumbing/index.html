Grease Trap Service

Restaurants and food service businesses need regular grease trap pumping and cleaning to stay compliant and avoid the exact kind of grease-clogged line that hydro jetting exists to clear. A trap that's overdue for service backs up gradually, first as slow kitchen drains, then as a full backup during a busy service period, which is the worst possible time to discover it.

Backflow Compliance For Commercial Properties

Commercial properties often have more backflow prevention devices than a typical home, covering irrigation, boilers, and any process water connections, each requiring its own annual certified test under most Colorado jurisdictions. Falling behind on this isn't just a compliance risk, a lapsed test can trigger water service restrictions from the utility.

Fixture Counts And Code Requirements

Commercial buildings are held to fixture-count requirements based on occupancy and use type, which residential plumbing doesn't need to account for. A renovation or tenant change can trigger a fresh code review of whether existing plumbing still meets current occupancy needs.

Restrooms open to the public also carry accessibility requirements for fixture height and clearance that a straightforward residential repair doesn't need to consider. We work with property managers and tenants directly when a lease requires plumbing work to be coordinated through a specific point of contact, keeping documentation clear for both parties. For multi-tenant buildings, we also flag clearly when a shared line issue is a landlord responsibility versus something specific to just one individual unit.
