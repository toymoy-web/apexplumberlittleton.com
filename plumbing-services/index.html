Common Questions

Do you charge for an estimate?

Free estimates apply to non-emergency work. Emergency calls still get an upfront price confirmed before any repair begins, just on a faster timeline given the urgency.

Can one technician handle multiple issues in the same visit?

Often yes, if you mention everything when you call so we can plan for it. Combining unrelated fixes into one visit is usually more efficient for everyone.

Do you offer the same services in all the areas you cover?

Yes, every service on this page is available across our full coverage area, including Ken Caryl, Columbine Valley, Bow Mar, Columbine, and Chatfield Bluffs.

What if my issue isn't listed here?

Call us directly. Most plumbing issues fall under one of these categories even if the specific wording doesn't match, and we're glad to clarify over the phone.

If your issue involves a specific neighborhood consideration, our service area page breaks down what's common in each community we serve, from Ken Caryl's own water district to Bow Mar's founding-era copper lines.
