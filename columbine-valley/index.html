Homes Built Around A 1955 Golf Course

According to the town's own history, Columbine Valley started as 17 families building homes around the newly formed Columbine Country Club in 1955. The town incorporated on its own four years later in 1959 to protect that identity from annexation by Denver or Littleton. With around 600 homes today, most of the town's housing stock dates from that founding era through the 1980s and 90s, making it one of the older, more established residential cores in this part of the service area.

What That Age Means For Plumbing

Homes from the 1955-1970 founding decades are old enough that original galvanized supply lines and aging clay sewer laterals are a real, recurring issue here, more so than in newer-built parts of the network's coverage area. Columbine Valley sits along the South Platte River Valley on the former 295-acre Heckendorf Farm site. The same soil conditions that made it good farmland can also mean shifting ground affecting older sewer lines over decades.

A Small Town That Chose To Stay Small

Columbine Valley has held at roughly 600 homes for decades by design, resisting the kind of rapid infill development that reshaped some neighboring areas. That stability means the town's original 1950s-60s housing stock hasn't been diluted by waves of newer construction the way some communities are. It's part of why founding-era plumbing issues are so consistently the top call here rather than one factor among many.

What We Get Called For Most

Whole-home repiping and sewer line repair top the list here, tied directly to the town's founding-era construction. Water heater replacement is a close second, since original and even second-generation units in the oldest homes are well past typical service life.

Common Questions From Columbine Valley Homeowners

Are Columbine Valley's water and sewer separate utilities?

Yes. Denver Water handles the water supply, while Platte Canyon Water and Sanitation District handles sewer service, a split worth knowing if you're troubleshooting a specific issue.

Is my home old enough to need repiping?

If your home dates from the town's founding era in the 1950s or 60s, original galvanized piping is a real possibility worth having evaluated, especially after a second or third leak in different spots.

Do you work on homes near the Country Club specifically?

Yes, we serve all of Columbine Valley, including the streets immediately surrounding Columbine Country Club.

Is soil movement near the river valley a real concern?

The South Platte River Valley setting does mean real soil movement over decades, which is a documented factor in shifted sewer joints in older lines here.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Columbine Valley?

Yes, the entire Town of Columbine Valley sits within the single 80123 ZIP code, and we cover every street from the Country Club core out to the town's borders with Bow Mar and the rest of Littleton.

Is emergency service available in Columbine Valley?

Yes, our 24/7 emergency line dispatches to Columbine Valley the same as anywhere else in our service area, day or night, weekends and holidays included.
