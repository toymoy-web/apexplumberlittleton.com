Who Actually Shows Up

Every technician we send works under Colorado's DORA plumbing licensing framework, and we background-check and train our team before they're dispatched to a customer's home unsupervised. You're not getting a subcontractor pulled from an app; you're getting someone accountable to the same business you called.

What We Actually Do

Littleton Apex Plumbing handles residential and commercial plumbing across Littleton, CO and the surrounding neighborhoods, from routine drain cleaning and faucet repair to full water heater replacement, sewer line work, and emergency response for burst pipes and active leaks. We work under Colorado's DORA plumbing licensing framework, and we're glad to confirm license details when you call.

What We Won't Do

We won't recommend a full replacement when a repair will genuinely hold, and we won't quote a job without seeing or fully understanding the actual scope first. If a job needs a specialist outside plumbing, like an electrician for an unrelated issue we notice, we'll say so honestly rather than attempting work outside our expertise.

How We Approach Pricing

We give an upfront price before any work begins, confirmed over the phone or on-site, so you know what a job costs before we touch anything. Free estimates apply to non-emergency work; for emergency calls, we still confirm a flat price before starting, just faster given the urgency. We don't believe in surprise line items once a job is already underway.

Why We Recommend What We Recommend

A repair recommendation from us means we think the repair will actually hold. If a fix won't hold, or if a bigger issue is likely to resurface the same problem in a few months, we say so directly rather than billing for a repair we don't believe in. That's true whether it means recommending a full water heater replacement instead of another repair, or a camera inspection before committing to a sewer line fix.

This approach costs us the occasional smaller job in the short term, when a customer would have accepted a repair we didn't think was the right call. We think it's worth it, since a customer who trusts our recommendation on a small job is more likely to call us again for the next one.

Licensing & Compliance

Our technicians work under Colorado's DORA plumbing licensing framework, and every installation follows the plumbing code adopted by the jurisdiction the work is performed in, commonly UPC or IPC depending on the specific municipality or special district. We're insured for residential and commercial work, and we're glad to provide verification when asked.

This matters more than it might seem, since our service area alone spans several different water and sanitation districts, each with its own infrastructure and, occasionally, its own local requirements. Knowing which district serves a given address is part of doing the job right, not an afterthought.
