Greenwood Village, ZIPs 80111, 80121, 80112 And 80110

Greenwood Village is a home rule city in Arapahoe County with 15,691 residents, according to the 2020 Census. It incorporated as a town in 1950 and adopted its home rule charter in 1968.

The city's own figures put its daytime population near 38,500, because so many people work in its business parks. We are based in Littleton, and Greenwood Village sits northeast of us, so we treat it as part of our regular coverage.

Offices, Estates, And Condos In One City

Greenwood Village includes part of the Denver Technological Center, which began growing in the 1970s. Mayor Freda Poundstone resisted Denver's efforts to annex more land for the Tech Center, and the 1974 Poundstone Amendment followed.

That mix shapes our work here. Office buildings need commercial plumbing and backflow compliance, while the residential side includes estate homes on larger lots and attached condos and townhomes near the Tech Center.
