Before You Call

What To Check First

No Hot Water

Check your water heater's breaker or gas valve first. If the pilot light is out on a gas unit, do not attempt to relight it yourself if you smell gas. Leave the area and call from outside.

Active Leak Or Burst Pipe

Shut off the main water valve, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Move valuables away from the water and photograph the damage for insurance before cleanup starts.

Slow Or Clogged Drain

Avoid chemical drain cleaners before a plumber arrives. They can obscure the real cause and make hydro jetting or camera inspection harder if the clog turns out to be a sewer line issue.

Suspected Slab Or Hidden Leak

Note your water bill trend and whether you hear running water with every fixture off. That detail alone often narrows a slab leak diagnosis before a technician even arrives.

Homeowner's insurance in Colorado typically covers a sudden burst pipe but not gradual leaks or wear-related failures. Confirm your policy's specifics before assuming a repair is covered, and keep photos and this call's invoice for any claim you file.
