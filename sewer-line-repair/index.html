What Actually Damages A Sewer Line

Littleton's expansive clay soil shifts with moisture, and that movement is a documented cause of offset or cracked joints in older clay and cast-iron sewer laterals. Mature trees in Old Town Littleton and the older sections of Columbine Valley send roots into these lines through the smallest gap, and once roots are established, they regrow inside the pipe within weeks of cabling alone.

A localized crack with root intrusion is different from a fully collapsed or bellied section, and the camera is what tells us which one we're dealing with. Treating a collapsed section like a simple root clog wastes money on repeat cabling visits that were never going to hold.

Spot Repair Vs. Trenchless Lining

A single cracked or offset joint, isolated to a short section, is usually fixed with a spot repair, excavating just that section rather than the whole line. When damage runs along a longer stretch, trenchless pipe lining installs a new pipe liner inside the old one without digging up the yard, which matters on properties with mature landscaping or a driveway sitting over the line.

Neither method fixes a fully collapsed line, and if the camera shows that, a full line replacement is the honest answer rather than a repair that won't hold pressure. Any repair method used has to meet the pipe material and slope requirements in your jurisdiction's adopted plumbing code, commonly UPC or IPC, not just physically stop the leak.

Repair Methods Compared

Spot Repair

  • Best for a single, isolated crack or joint.
  • Involves excavating just the damaged section.
  • Faster and less disruptive for small, localized damage.
  • Not a fix for damage spread across a longer run.

Trenchless Lining

  • Installs a new liner inside the existing pipe.
  • No digging up the yard, driveway, or landscaping.
  • Works well for root intrusion along a longer stretch.
  • Not suitable for a fully collapsed section.

Sewer Smell In A Bathroom

A smell near one bathroom does not always mean a broken sewer. A trap that has dried out, or one that does not hold water, lets gas rise from the drain. Running water in an unused fixture for a minute often settles it. If the smell persists, a vent problem or a cracked line below the floor is the next suspect.

What About Septic Systems?

Our sewer work covers the line between your house and the city main, plus the drains inside the home. Septic tanks and drain fields sit outside that scope, so ask before booking if your property is not on city sewer. For clogs inside the house, drain cleaning is usually the first step.

Common Questions

Why does my sewer backup keep coming back at the same spot?

A backup that recurs at the same location is usually root intrusion or a shifted joint rather than routine buildup. A camera inspection confirms the cause so you're not paying for the same cabling visit repeatedly.

Will you have to dig up my yard?

Not always. Trenchless lining repairs many sewer line problems without excavation. A camera inspection tells us whether your specific damage qualifies before we recommend an approach.

How is spot repair different from a full replacement?

Spot repair fixes an isolated section of damage. A full replacement is only needed when the camera shows the line has collapsed or deteriorated along most of its length.

Is a sewer backup an emergency?

A backup affecting multiple drains or a lowest fixture in the house is treated as same-day priority, since it usually means no drain in the house is working.

My bathroom smells like sewage. Is the sewer line broken?

Not always. A dry or leaking trap is the most common cause. If a minute of running water does not fix it, a camera check shows whether the line or the vent is at fault.

Can you find where my sewer line runs?

Tell the dispatcher you need the route located. The camera visit is the right place to ask for it, because the route and depth matter before any excavation or landscaping.

Do you work on septic tanks?

No. We work on city sewer connections and on drains inside the house. Properties on septic should ask about that before booking.

How much does sewer line repair cost?

It depends on the damage type, the depth of the line, and what sits on top of it. The camera result lets the dispatcher give a clear quote before any digging.
