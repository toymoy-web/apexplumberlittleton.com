Where Goddard Sits In Littleton

Goddard is the name Homes.com uses for a Littleton neighborhood in Arapahoe County, and it shares its name with Goddard Middle School. Homes.com places it just outside Historic Downtown Littleton and lists streets such as South Lowell Boulevard, West Berry Place, West Powers Place, West Aksarben Avenue, Bellflower Drive and South Meade Street. It lists ZIP 80123.

The City of Littleton's land use map shows Wynetka Ponds open space, and Homes.com describes it as in the neighborhood. We found no official boundary. ZIP 80123 is shared with other communities, including Bow Mar, which has its own page on this site, so this page covers the Goddard name and not Bow Mar.

Verified Local Facts

Each card below is attributed to a source listed at the bottom of this page. Figures from real estate sites are aggregator numbers and not surveys.

Parks And Open Space

Homes.com lists Berry Park, Valley Vista Park, Harlow Park and Wynetka Ponds near the neighborhood.

Housing Figures

Homes.com lists a median year built of 1970. It says most homes date from the 1970s and 1980s, with some newer homes from the last decade.

Home And Lot Size

Homes.com gives 2,633 sq ft as the average single-family size and 12,196 sq ft as the median lot. Both are aggregator figures.

Housing Mix

Homes.com describes ranch homes, larger contemporary homes and townhouses, so there is no single housing profile.

What The Homes Look Like

The mix of housing types means there is no single plumbing profile here. A ranch from the 1970s, a newer contemporary home and a townhouse each call for different questions. We have not inspected the area and make no claim about pipe materials or sewer condition for Goddard as a whole.

What the larger figures do suggest is more fixtures and more outdoor plumbing than a compact home would have. That shapes the questions below. If your house is a townhouse, some of them will not apply.

Plumbing For Larger Homes On Larger Lots

General plumbing guidance, not a local finding. The points below apply to many Colorado homes. We list them because they fit what is known about this area, and we do not claim they describe your street.

More Bathrooms, More DemandA larger home puts more fixtures on one water heater. If hot water runs out, capacity or a failing element may be the cause. A technician can size a unit to real use.
Hard WaterFront Range water leaves scale on heaters and fixtures. A softener or filter is a common addition when a heater is replaced.
Outdoor LinesHose bibs and irrigation lines on larger lots face winter exposure. Shut them off and drain them before the first freeze.
Longer Drain RunsLonger runs and more bends give debris more places to catch. Repeat clogs in one line call for a camera check.
Tankless OptionsTankless units suit some homes and not others. Gas line size and venting decide whether the swap is simple.

Common Questions From Goddard Homeowners

Do you serve the Goddard area?

Yes. We are based in Littleton and serve this part of the city. Call (303) 552-3896 with your street address and we will confirm coverage.

Is Goddard the same as Bow Mar?

No. They share ZIP 80123, but Bow Mar is a separate incorporated town with its own page on this site. Either way, a call settles coverage for your address.

How do I size a water heater for a larger home?

Size depends on how many people and bathrooms use hot water at once. A technician can recommend a tank or tankless unit based on your actual use.

When should I shut off outdoor water lines?

Before the first hard freeze. Shut off the supply to hose bibs, drain the line and disconnect hoses.

Are Goddard homes old enough to need repiping?

We cannot say that for the area. Homes.com lists a median build year of 1970, but each house has its own repair history. A repipe is decided after inspection and testing, not by age alone.
