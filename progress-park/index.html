Where Progress Park Sits In Littleton

Progress Park is a city park, and real estate and map sites such as Homes.com and Walk Score use the name for the neighborhood around it. Homes.com places Belleview Park and the Big Dry Creek Trail on the north side. It puts the Littleton Community Trail along the east, Littleton Boulevard to the south and Slaughterhouse Gulch Park to the southwest. It lists ZIP codes 80120 and 80121.

Those edges come from one aggregator and are not an official boundary. If your address sits near one of them, call us and we will confirm coverage. This is part of our normal Littleton service area.

Verified Local Facts

Each card below is attributed to a source listed at the bottom of this page. Figures from real estate sites are aggregator numbers and not surveys.

Parks Nearby

Homes.com lists Belleview Park, Cornerstone Park and Slaughterhouse Gulch Park around the neighborhood. It describes Cornerstone Park as linking Progress Park to Belleview Park.

Housing Figures

Homes.com lists 1,062 homes, a median year built of 1959 and a median lot of 8,276 sq ft.

Home Size

Homes.com gives 1,608 sq ft as the average single-family size, which it calls below the national average.

Variety

Homes.com says smaller ranch and midcentury modern homes predominate. It also mentions a few prewar Craftsman homes and Tudor-style homes on larger lots on the east side.

What The Homes Look Like

Averages can hide a lot of variety. The same description notes that some homes have no garage and that lot sizes change from one side of the neighborhood to the other. We have not inspected the area, so we make no claim about pipe material or sewer condition for Progress Park as a whole.

What the figures do suggest is compact homes. In a smaller house the plumbing is gathered in a small area. One failure can affect the kitchen, bath and laundry together, which is why a clear shutoff and a quick diagnosis matter.

Plumbing In Compact Postwar Homes

General plumbing guidance, not a local finding. The points below apply to many Colorado homes. We list them because they fit what is known about this area, and we do not claim they describe your street.

One Bathroom, Little MarginIn a home with a single bath, a failed toilet or tub drain disrupts the whole household. Know your shutoffs and call early.
Tight Utility SpacesThe water heater may sit in a closet, a crawlspace or living space. Available room and venting decide which replacement units fit.
Homes Without A GarageWhen a heater sits inside the living area, a leak reaches finished floors fast. A drain pan and a known shutoff help limit damage.
Fixture UpgradesReplacing a worn toilet, faucet or tub is a common project. Matching the rough-in size avoids opening more wall than needed.
Shared Drain LinesKitchen, bath and laundry drains often meet in one line in a small house. A clog that shows up in two places usually sits downstream of both.
Planning A Bath RefreshSwapping a tub for a shower or updating a vanity is easier when the supply and drain locations are checked first.

Before The Technician Arrives

Find the shutoffs

Locate the main valve and the small valve under the affected fixture.

Describe the symptom

Note whether the problem is a leak, a clog, low pressure or no hot water.

Clear a path

Move items near the fixture, heater or utility closet.

Share the fixture details

A photo of the toilet, faucet or heater label helps us bring matching parts.

Common Questions From Progress Park Homeowners

Do you serve homes near Progress Park?

Yes. We are based in Littleton and serve this part of the city. Call (303) 552-3896 with your street address and we will confirm coverage.

Are Progress Park homes old enough to need repiping?

We cannot say that for the area. Homes.com lists a median build year of 1959, but each house has its own repair history. A repipe is decided after inspection and testing, not by age alone.

Can a small utility closet fit a new water heater?

Often yes, but it depends on the unit size and venting. A technician measures the space and checks venting before recommending a model.

Why do two drains in my house clog together?

Drains that meet in one line will back up together when the clog sits downstream of both. A drain cleaning or camera check usually finds it.

Do you fix just one fixture or do whole-bath projects?

Both. We repair single toilets, faucets and drains, and we install replacement showers and tubs. Call and describe the job for a quote.
